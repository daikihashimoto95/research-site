量子多体系の振る舞いをどこまで厳密に導けるのか、また、その結論にどのような仮定が本質的なのかに興味があります。現在は、対称性や代数構造に着目しながら、孤立量子系の熱平衡化とその例外となる量子多体スカー（Quantum Many-Body Scars）を研究しています。

I am interested in what can be rigorously established about the behavior of quantum many-body systems, and which assumptions are essential to those conclusions. My current research focuses on thermalization in isolated quantum systems and quantum many-body scars as exceptions to thermalization, with particular attention to symmetries and algebraic structures.
